# Materna Medical

> Materna Medical is a Mountain View, California women's health medical device company
> building an OBGYN platform around pelvic floor health. It sells regulated physical
> devices and clinical evidence — not software. There is no Materna Medical API.

Materna Medical publishes **no developer program, no public API, no SDK, no webhooks and no
machine-readable API contract** (no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP server or A2A agent
card). Probed 2026-08-25: every OpenAPI, GraphQL, MCP and `/.well-known/` path on
www.maternamedical.com and www.hellomilli.com returns 404, and api., developer., docs., app.,
platform., mcp. and portal..maternamedical.com are NXDOMAIN. If you are an agent looking for
an integration surface here, there is none to call — use the human pages below.

## Products

- [Milli](https://www.hellomilli.com/vaginal-dilator/): An expanding vaginal dilator that
  widens one millimeter at a time with optional vibration, for vaginismus, dyspareunia,
  postpartum recovery and menopause-related painful sex. Launched 2019 as a wellness trainer;
  FDA-cleared for over-the-counter sale in 2023. Sold direct-to-consumer and through OBGYN and
  pelvic floor physical therapy practices. HSA/FSA eligible.
- Ellora: An investigational device used during labor, intended to reduce pelvic floor muscle
  injury during vaginal delivery. Studied in the randomized EASE trial across US hospitals.
  Not commercially available; assigned ICD-10-PCS technology code XU7 effective April 2026.
